# Cooperative Quote Acceptance

**Drop-in source for Documenso.** Import as a new template named
`Cooperative Quote Acceptance` and set its id as
`DOCUMENSO_TEMPLATE_COOPERATIVE_QUOTE` in Dokploy.

**Two things the template must carry or the API fails at the moment a
client clicks sign:**

1. **One recipient placeholder** — the client signer. The invite flow
   throws a named error when a template has no placeholder slot, and
   this will do the same.
2. **A signature field placed on page one**, in the box marked below.
   A template with no signature field renders a document the client can
   open and cannot sign.

**Why this document is short, and why it does not list the crew.** A
Documenso template is a static document. The crew, their rates, their
deliverables and their timelines differ on every quote and already
live on the proposal page, which the client has in front of them when
they sign. Reproducing them here would mean either a template per
quote or a table that cannot be filled. So this accepts a quote by
reference, and the reference resolves to the exact version the client
read.

**Fields to place in Documenso:** client signer name, client company,
quote reference, selected lead Builder, date, signature.

### 1. What is being accepted

The Client accepts the Cooperative Quote identified below, as
presented on the Cooperative's proposal page at the time of signing.
That page states the proposed Builders, each Builder's terms, each
Builder's deliverables, and each Builder's availability. It is the
operative description of the engagement and is incorporated here by
reference.

**Quote reference:** _______________________________________________

**Client:** _______________________________________________________

### 2. This is a quote, not an invoice

The figures shown are the Builders' proposed terms. Where a price is
given as a range, or marked negotiable, the final amount is settled
with the Cooperative before contracting and is not fixed by this
acceptance. No amount becomes payable on signature.

Hourly engagements are billed as delivered. Nothing in this document
commits the Client to a minimum number of hours.

### 3. What happens next

On signature, the Cooperative begins the engagement paperwork: a
statement of work with the Client, and an engagement confirmation with
the Lead Builder and any other Builders the Client has selected. Work
does not begin until that paperwork is executed.

### 4. Authority

The person signing represents that they are authorised to accept this
proposal on behalf of the Client. Signature creates no personal
obligation on the signer.

### 5. Confidentiality

The proposal, including Builder identities, rates and the
Cooperative's margin, is confidential to the Client and is not to be
shared outside the Client's organisation without the Cooperative's
written consent.

Builders are engaged through the Cooperative. The Client agrees not to
solicit or contract any proposed Builder directly, outside the
Cooperative, for a period of twelve months from the date of this
acceptance.

### 6. Withdrawal

The Cooperative may withdraw or revise a quote at any time before this
acceptance is signed. After signature, changes to crew, scope or price
are made by agreement between the parties.
