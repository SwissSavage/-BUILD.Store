# FUTURE MODERN
## Talent Partner Agreement

This agreement (the "Agreement") is now entered by and between
FUTURE MODERN (the "Company") and the undersigned goods or services
provider ("ARTIST"), (all together referred to as "the parties").

WHEREAS, the Company has developed Products (applications,
databases, designs, graphics, etc) and corresponding Services as
well as intends to in future (the "Products", "Products and
Services") and holds ownership of all intellectual property rights
in these current and future Products;

WHEREAS, the Company desires to curate, deal, sell, distribute,
market, or otherwise represent ("curate") the ARTIST's artwork,
assets, products, goods, services, labor, skills, know-how, and
expertise ("WORK") using the Products for sales ("Sale",
"Transaction"), which also may include recurring components; and

WHEREAS, the Company and the ARTIST desire to enter into an
arrangement whereby the Company and ARTIST will share the profits
realized from the sale of WORK(s) due to the Products and Services,
and efforts of the Company according to the terms and conditions
herein,

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and
promises made by the parties hereto, the Company and the ARTIST
(individually each a "Party" and collectively, the "Parties")
covenant and agree as follows:

In exchange for the curation, distribution, and sale of ARTIST's
WORK in a Company or online ("curation"), the parties now agree to
the following:

1. The Company will present, display, or exhibit the ARTIST's
WORK(s) at an exhibition at the FUTURE MODERN MUSEUM, located dually
at a physical location and in the metaverse; or online on future
modern's $BUILD STORE online storefront; or online on the FUTURE
MODERN SERVICES PORTAL online directory; or at any other in-person,
online, or onchain venues or platforms where the Company currently
presents, displays, or exhibits WORK(s) or will in the future.
Before the Company places any WORK at these locations, the ARTIST
will be informed and must approve the placement.

2. The WORK(s) curated, marketed, produced, presented, displayed,
exhibited, and represented by future modern under this Agreement are
from now on collectively referred to as the "WORK" and refer to
those implemented or proposed artworks, products, services, assets,
forms of media, or other WORK(s) originally rendered for sale by
ARTIST, submitted by ARTIST to FUTURE MODERN, and accepted by FUTURE
MODERN for curation.

3. In exchange for FUTURE MODERN's curation of ARTIST'S WORK, ARTIST
grants FUTURE MODERN the right to represent ARTIST in the subsequent
sale and further representation of the WORK by FUTURE MODERN.

4. TERM: The term of this Agreement begins when ARTIST'S WORK is
submitted to FUTURE MODERN for curation. ARTIST can complete
submission either electronically or by any other means. Upon written
mutual agreement between FUTURE MODERN and ARTIST, any WORK that
ARTIST has rendered but which is not curated by FUTURE MODERN may
also become WORK to be curated by FUTURE MODERN under the terms of
this Agreement.

This Agreement shall have an initial term of 2 years from its
execution date, and shall continue in force from year to year after
that, but only so long as such continuance is approved at least
annually by both parties in writing.

5. If any Buyer is introduced to ARTIST'S WORK through FUTURE
MODERN, any eventual sale resulting from that introduction shall be
subject to the terms of this Agreement.

6. COMMISSION: The following payment terms will apply to all sales
of and proceeds from each subject WORK curated by FUTURE MODERN: 85%
(payment to ARTIST), 15% (commission to FUTURE MODERN). This
includes but is not limited to

- any proceeds resulting from the release, distribution, or sale of
  WORK, including but not limited to primary or secondary sales of
  WORK; token airdrops; compensation deals including employment
  agreements, sponsorships, wages, and salaries; and prize money,
  perks, or bonuses;
- any tokens, shares, stake, or any form of assets, ownership, or
  equity retained in any partial ownership or equity sale of WORK;
- any other funds, earnings, or proceeds generated from any partial
  or whole ownership or equity sale of WORK as described above.

6a. If FUTURE MODERN successfully refers the ARTIST to permanent,
full-time employment, then 6.) will only apply to the ARTIST's first
year's earnings. A successful referral to permanent, full-time
employment shall mean the ARTIST accepts a permanent full-time
employment offer introduced to the ARTIST by FUTURE MODERN for which
the employer did not consider the ARTIST before this agreement.

**6b. BASE RATE AND BONUS MARGIN (v2 addition).** The 85/15 split
in Section 6 is the headline structure and is not changed.
ARTIST's 85% share is composed of two components:

- **Base rate.** The guaranteed portion of ARTIST's share, paid on
  acceptable delivery of scoped contribution. Base rate is not
  contingent on performance signals beyond acceptance of the WORK
  itself.
- **Bonus margin.** The remainder of ARTIST's share, released based
  on ARTIST's real contribution to the delivered WORK as measured
  by FUTURE MODERN's attribution, peer-review, client-satisfaction,
  and MVP-score signals.

Where ARTIST's contribution meets or exceeds the scoped expectation,
the full bonus margin releases to ARTIST alongside the base rate.
Where ARTIST's contribution falls materially short, the unreleased
portion of the bonus margin is redistributed by the platform in
this priority order:

- To contributors who had to cover for ARTIST's shortfall, in
  proportion to the coverage they provided; and
- Where no covering contributor exists and the shortfall produced
  client dissatisfaction, the unreleased bonus margin — together
  with some or all of FUTURE MODERN's 15% commission on that WORK
  — is rebated to the client as a concession.

The base rate / bonus margin split for each WORK is set at
engagement acceptance and is disclosed to ARTIST as part of the
Statement of Work. This mechanic is not punitive: ARTIST always
receives the base rate on acceptable delivery, and ARTIST always
receives the portion of the bonus margin that ARTIST earned by
real output. The mechanic exists so that bad actors do not collect
on contributions they did not make, covering contributors are made
whole, and clients on the receiving end of a shortfall get a
good-faith remedy. Bonus-margin determinations are recorded on the
platform and are subject to the appeal path defined in FUTURE
MODERN's cooperative covenant.

By signing this document, ARTIST agrees to each stipulation outlined
in this Agreement.

7. FUTURE MODERN reserves the right to determine payment terms

8. ARTIST(s) outside the United States are responsible for any

9. ARTIST retains ownership of all WORK represented by FUTURE MODERN
until such WORK is sold under the terms of this Agreement or
otherwise. ARTIST retains all intellectual property and copyright to
all consigned WORK in perpetuity, as governed by law. FUTURE MODERN
shall take the steps necessary to ensure that the ARTIST's copyright
notice is projected and included in all documentation.

**9a. Engagement-type IP allocation (v2 clarification).** The
retention of ARTIST IP under Section 9 applies to Curation
Engagements — the default configuration of this Agreement, in which
FUTURE MODERN represents ARTIST's own WORK. For engagement
configurations outside pure Curation, the following allocation
applies and shall be identified on the engagement's Statement of
Work or equivalent:

- **Client-paid engagements.** Where the engagement exists to
  deliver work product to a paying third-party client of FUTURE
  MODERN, ownership of that work product vests in the paying client
  upon creation, on work-made-for-hire terms; to the extent it does
  not qualify as a work made for hire, ARTIST irrevocably assigns
  all right, title, and interest in the work product to FUTURE
  MODERN, which assigns to the client per FUTURE MODERN's
  client-side agreement.
- **Internal FUTURE MODERN builds.** Where the engagement is an
  internal FUTURE MODERN build with no external paying client,
  ownership vests in FUTURE MODERN upon creation, on the same
  work-made-for-hire / assignment terms.
- **Talent-development engagements.** Where the engagement exists to
  represent, promote, or develop ARTIST's own product, brand, or IP
  (the default Curation configuration), ownership remains with
  ARTIST as set out in Section 9 above.

10. The Company will establish the sales price for each WORK with

12. FUTURE MODERN will maintain backups of any digital WORK(s) via

FUTURE MODERN will not share backup copies of WORK with third

13. MODIFICATIONS: All modifications of this Agreement must be in

### 17. Conduct Standards (v2 addition)

ARTIST acknowledges that FUTURE MODERN's client, buyer, and partner
relationships are the foundation of the business FUTURE MODERN
operates and that ARTIST's conduct during any engagement reflects
directly on FUTURE MODERN. During any engagement involving a
third-party client, buyer, or venue introduced through or handled by
FUTURE MODERN, ARTIST agrees:

**(a) Client-facing communications route through FUTURE MODERN.**
All communications with the client, buyer, or venue — including
email, chat, calls, invoices, timesheets, quotes, deposit or payment
inquiries, and reply-all messages on threads that include the client
— will go through or be visibly copied to the FUTURE MODERN account
owner. ARTIST will not send billing, timesheets, quotes, deposit
requests, or payment reminders directly to the client without prior
written authorization from FUTURE MODERN.

**(b) Rate and commission lock at engagement acceptance.** The rate,
sales price, or commission structure agreed at engagement acceptance
under this Agreement (including under Section 6 and Section 10) is
locked for the duration of the engagement. ARTIST will not propose,
invoice, or discuss a different rate, price, or commission structure
with the client. Adjustments, when needed, are handled
agency-to-ARTIST per Section 13, never ARTIST-to-client.

**(c) FUTURE MODERN sets commercial terms.** Payment terms, deposit
requirements, late-payment interest, kill fees, retainers, credit
terms, and other commercial arrangements with the client are set by
FUTURE MODERN. ARTIST will not propose, negotiate, or discuss such
terms with clients directly.

**(d) Representation of FUTURE MODERN.** ARTIST will represent
FUTURE MODERN as the contract of record and the delivery entity on
the engagement. Public or client-facing minimization of FUTURE
MODERN's role (including describing FUTURE MODERN as "just a
broker," "an intermediary," or similar) is a material breach.

**(e) Engagement economics confidential to the client.** ARTIST's
proceeds, FUTURE MODERN's commission or markup, and the client's
total contract value are not disclosed to the client. ARTIST may
discuss rates with other artists and contractors; FUTURE MODERN
operates labor-first and supports cross-ARTIST transparency. Client
disclosure remains prohibited.

**Consequence.** Any single material breach of subsections (a)
through (e) is grounds for FUTURE MODERN, at its sole discretion, to
(i) issue a written notice of concern, (ii) apply a compliance
penalty on the platform record, (iii) withhold or forfeit any
unearned bonus or holdback, and/or (iv) terminate the engagement
effective immediately without further compensation for services not
yet performed. Repeated breach ends the engagement without further
notice.

## Confidentiality, Non-Competition, and Non-Circumvention.

The parties shall not, without the advanced written consent of the
other party, in any fashion, form, or manner, either directly or
indirectly:

- Disclose or communicate to any third party any information
  relating to the Company's business or the Products including
  (but not limited to) customer lists, price points, or marketing
  plans (the "Confidential Information");
- Duplicate any Confidential information;
- Use any Confidential Information other than solely for the benefit
  of the other party; or; assist a third party in using any
  Confidential Information in any manner but solely for the other
  party's benefit.
- Encourage, solicit or induce, or in any manner attempt to solicit
  or induce, any individual or entity revealed to be employed by,
  as an agent of, or a service provider to, the other party to
  terminate such person's employment, agency or service, as the
  case may be, with the other party.
- Engage in Competition with the other party by soliciting business
  of the type performed by the Company, which the party did not
  engage in before the AGREEMENT, especially from any individual
  or entity for whom the party prospected, or provided services or
  products for the term of this executed agreement;
- Resell any form of media deemed WORK under contract currently
  managed by the parties;
- Directly or indirectly, contact, deal with, or otherwise become
  involved with any entity or any other entities or parties
  introduced, directly or indirectly, by or through the other
  party, its officers, directors, agents, or associates, to avoid
  the payment to the other party of profits, fees or otherwise. If
  such circumvention shall occur the party shall be entitled to
  any commissions due under this agreement or relating to such
  transaction.

**Period (v2 addition).** The obligations in the four bullets above
covering solicitation-of-personnel, competition, resale, and
circumvention shall apply for the term of this Agreement and for
twenty-four (24) months after its conclusion. For engagements
identified in the Statement of Work as **contract-to-hire**
arrangements — where direct handoff of ARTIST to the client is the
intended outcome of the engagement — the post-Agreement period is
twelve (12) months instead. A client, buyer, or venue who
independently and unprompted contacts ARTIST is not a violation,
provided ARTIST notifies FUTURE MODERN promptly and routes the work
through FUTURE MODERN if it proceeds.
